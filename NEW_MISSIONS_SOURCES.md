## Models and representations
- NEAR: [NASA 3D model](https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/near-shoemaker/), original GLB downloaded from NASA.
- InSight: [NASA / Brian E. Kumanchik InSight Cruise Lander](https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/insight-cruise-lander/), arm deployed configuration. The model depicts the spacecraft after the solar panels and arm have deployed; the replay uses this same configuration throughout and explicitly says so. The seismometer appears as a simple illustration; the arm itself is not animated.
- Apollo 15 Falcon: reuses the existing NASA Apollo Lunar Module model as an approximation. The lunar rover is constructed procedurally for this prototype, not a precise CAD model.
- Eros terrain and spacecraft landing orientation, Hadley–Apennine terrain, and all descent trajectories are illustrative, not telemetry. The Eros cruise direction is illustrative rather than an ephemeris. The spacecraft Sun-facing Earth view and Mars/Sun geometry are approximations in this prototype.
- The Drive folder contains an InSight GLB and a research document. A smaller official NASA InSight model was used for the site; the Drive GLB was not incorporated. The inspected research document concerned Kepler and Ingenuity, so it was not treated as a source for the three new mission stories.

## Shortcuts
`?go=apollo15`, `?go=near`, `?go=insight`, with optional `&motion=<seconds>` to pause the reconstruction. The three missions use compressed historical time and display educational narration.
