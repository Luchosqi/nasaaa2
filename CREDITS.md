# Credits

## Imagery and data
- **Star sky / Milky Way**: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, *Deep Star Maps 2020* (Hipparcos-2, Tycho-2, Gaia DR2), https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851. Tone-mapped from `starmap_2020_8k.exr`. Public domain.
- **Moon color and relief**: NASA SVS *CGI Moon Kit* (LRO LROC color mosaic, LOLA elevation `ldem_4`), https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720. Normal map derived from LOLA. Public domain.
- **Mars, Earth (day, night lights, clouds), Sun, Mercury, Venus, Jupiter, Saturn (+ rings), Uranus, Neptune textures**: Solar System Scope, https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/, licensed **CC BY 4.0**, based on NASA data.

## 3D models
NASA 3D Resources, https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources (public domain):
- Apollo Lunar Module (only the descent stage is shown, as on the Moon)
- Mars Exploration Rover – Opportunity (MER-B)
- Ingenuity Mars Helicopter
- Voyager Probe (B)

Procedural (built in code for this project): the Saturn V, Delta II, Atlas V and Titan IIIE/Centaur rockets, the Apollo Command/Service Module, the Mars cruise stages and aeroshells, Sojourner, the Mars Pathfinder lander, the Apollo 11 EASEP experiments and flag, terrain and rocks.
